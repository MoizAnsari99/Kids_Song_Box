Kallu Madari aaya re,
Dug-dugi apni laaya re,
Dum dum dum woh bajaye,
Bachchon ko sab paas bulaye.

Bandar chhota naache re,
Kood-kood kar gaaye re,
Kallu bole, “Taali bajao!”
Sab bachche bole, “Phir se dikhao!”

Dum dum dum, dug-dugi baje,
Khushi ke rang har taraf saje,
Kallu Madari hansata jaaye,
Bachchon ke mann ko bhaaye.

Bandar pehne laal topi,
Chalta jaise chhoti si fauji,
Kabhi woh baithe, kabhi woh naache,
Bachche hans-hans taali bajaye.

Kallu Madari aaya re,
Khushiyon ka mela laaya re,
Dum dum dum woh bajaye,
Sabko hansakar ghar ko jaaye.
